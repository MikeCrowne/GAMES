Six Fires Stand Between You and the Crown

The old kingdom road is the last surviving artery between the frontier town of Dunmere and the Ember Kingdom. The woods around it are haunted by creatures drawn to the soul-flames in the torches. You are Caelan, an oathsworn warden escorting the Heartflame Ember to the capital before the woods consume the realm.

Reach six towns, claim stronger blessings in each refuge, survive ambushes from cursed beasts in the woods, and deliver the Heartflame Ember to the kingdom at dawn. Click into the game to capture the mouse and look around freely.
