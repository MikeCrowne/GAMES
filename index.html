Modern 3D fantasy platformer

Arcane Hocus 3D

A true 3D reimagining of the DOS-era magic-platformer vibe: free-look camera, 3D traversal, checkpoint banners, forgiving movement, and more readable modern UX.

How to play

  • Mouse look: Click the canvas to lock the cursor, then move the mouse
  • Move: WASD / Arrow keys
  • Jump: Space
  • Cast spell: Left click / F
  • Air dash: Shift
  • Pause: P
  • Respawn: R

Modern upgrades

  • Real 3D mouse-look exploration and aiming
  • Checkpoint banners with fast recovery
  • Assist mode for bigger jumps and lower incoming damage
  • Readable portal compass and quest feedback
  • Arcade-style combo boosts for aggressive crystal routes
